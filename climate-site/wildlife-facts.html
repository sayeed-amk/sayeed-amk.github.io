Fact: Did you know?

Koala fun fact

Koalas don't drink much water—they usually get it from eucalyptus leaves. But during heatwaves, they struggle to survive because leaves dry out, and they have to come down from trees to search for water.

Lizards fun fact

Reptiles like lizards often can't escape urban heat because they live on hard surfaces like roads or rooftops.

Frogs fun fact

Frogs depend on moist environments to survive and reproduce. But during long dry periods caused by heatwaves, wetlands shrink, making it hard for frogs to find shelter and lay eggs.

Flying fox fun fact

Flying foxes are very sensitive to extreme heat. During a 2020 heatwave in Queensland, hundreds died as they overheated and fell from trees, unable to cool themselves.

Birds fun fact

Native Birds and Water Shortage. Many native birds rely on puddles and small streams for drinking and bathing. As temperatures rise and water sources dry up, they travel further and face higher risks from predators and cars.

Urban Animals fun fact

Urban Animals Seeking Shelter. Animals like possums and echidnas are entering urban homes or sheds more often to escape the heat. But without access to water or food, many become weak or dehydrated.

Corals fun fact

Corals are animals, not plants! They rely on tiny algae living inside them for food and color. When ocean water gets too hot, the algae leave, causing coral bleaching — which can lead to coral death and leave fish without homes.
