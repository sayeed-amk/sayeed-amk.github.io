Intro Section: Heatwave

Heatwaves are stretches of very hot weather that last for days. In Brisbane, they are happening more often and getting hotter because of climate change. The recent hottest temperature in Brisbane was 37.4°C (99.3°F) on January 16, 2025. Occasional heatwaves can push temperatures up to 40°C.

Brisbane is experiencing more frequent and intense heatwaves as global temperatures rise. These extreme heat events pose significant risks to human health, infrastructure, and our local wildlife.

Tips for survival from Heatwaves

Stay Hydrated - Drink plenty of water
Seek Cool Places - Stay indoors with air conditioning when possible
Dress Light - Wear lightweight clothing
Avoid Peak Sun (10AM–4PM)
Help Wildlife - Provide water and shade
