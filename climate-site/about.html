ABOUT

Purpose

This website was created to raise awareness about the local impacts of climate change in Brisbane. It focuses on heatwaves, wildlife disruption, and greenhouse gas emissions. The site aims to educate, empower, and connect residents with meaningful actions they can take for a sustainable future.

Who Supports This Website?

This platform was developed by Griffith University students as part of a Design Thinking in IT course, in collaboration with Brisbane City Council. It is designed to help communities better understand and respond to climate-related challenges.

Our Goals

  • Inform the public about Brisbane's climate risks
  • Share practical strategies for coping with heatwaves
  • Promote the protection of local wildlife
  • Encourage everyday eco-friendly behaviors
  • Connect residents with local sustainability resources

Why It Matters to Brisbane

Brisbane is experiencing rising temperatures, more frequent heatwaves, and ecological stress. These changes impact our health, infrastructure, and local species. Taking informed action now helps build a stronger, more climate-resilient Brisbane for future generations.

What Can You Do?

Explore our tips, learn about climate risks in your suburb, and discover small actions that make a big difference. Whether it's adjusting your home energy use or supporting wildlife, every step counts.
